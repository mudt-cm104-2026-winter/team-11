PyPython

PyPython is a simple web-based Snake game, powered by a Python backend and featuring a snake designed to resemble a real python.The game offers two modes: Classic Mode, where hitting a wall ends the game, and Teleport Mode, where the snake reappears on the opposite side of the screen upon hitting a wall. Both modes feature their own Top 10 leaderboards, displaying the usernames and scores of the ten highest-scoring players.
